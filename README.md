# Smart-Elevator-Service-Management-System-Company
The main goal of this project is to design a smart and user-friendly Software-based system that helps people easily book elevator installation, repair, or maintenance services online.  Through this system, users can quickly connect with available technicians, track their service status in real-time, make payments and give feedback after service.


Requirements from the " Smart Elevator Service & Management System Company"

The elevator company aims to modernize and expand its service management by implementing a smart web-based system. They require a platform that allows customers to book elevator installation or repair services online, track technician status, view service history, receive real-time notifications, and make secure payments. The company also wants an admin panel to manage service requests, assign technicians based on location and availability, monitor performance, and generate business analytics reports to improve service delivery and customer satisfaction.


Summary of Key Requirements:
1. Online service booking (installation/repair/maintenance).
2. Customer login and service history tracking.
3. Technician tracking and job assignment.
4. Real-time status updates and notifications (SMS/email).
5. Online payment and invoice system.
6. Admin dashboard for managing technicians and services.
7. Reports and analytics for business growth.
8. Customer feedback and rating system.



Requirement Analysis
Based on the project requirements, we have analyzed the needs of the system in detail. The analysis below helps us understand what the system must do and how it will work.

i. Functional Requirements
These are the main functions that the system must perform.
1.	Online Service Booking:
Customers can book elevator installation, repair, or maintenance services through an online form.
2.	Customer Registration and Login:
Users can create accounts, log in securely, and view their service history anytime.
3.	Technician Management:
Admin can add, update, or remove technician profiles and assign them to service requests based on area and skill.
4.	Real-Time Tracking:
Customers can see the live status of their requested service and track the technician’s movement.
5.	Notification System:
The system sends notifications via SMS or email for service updates, confirmations, or completion.
6.	Online Payment and Invoice:
Customers can pay securely online and download invoices after completing the service.
7.	Admin Dashboard:
Admin can manage users, services, technicians, and view overall performance reports.
8.	Reports and Analytics:
The system generates monthly and yearly reports about service statistics and technician performance.
9.	Customer Feedback and Rating:
After service completion, users can rate and review their experience to help improve quality.



ii. Non-Functional Requirements
These describe how the system should perform rather than what it should do.
1.	Performance:
The system should load pages quickly and handle multiple users at the same time.
2.	Security:
All sensitive data must be encrypted, and secure login systems will be implemented.
3.	Reliability:
The system should be available 24/7 with minimum downtime.
4.	Scalability:
It should be easy to add more technicians, users, or features in the future.
5.	Usability:
The interface should be easy for all types of users to understand and operate.
6.	Maintainability:
The code will be modular so that future updates and bug fixes can be done easily.
